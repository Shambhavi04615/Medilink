How MediLink Works

Medicines are tracked from manufacturing to distribution using blockchain. Each batch is registered, verified, and authenticated through QR codes scanned by pharmacists and customers to ensure complete transparency.

Manufacturers

Create batches, generate QR codes, and store proof on blockchain.

Pharmacists

Scan QR, verify authenticity, and add medicines to inventory.

Customers

Verify medicines instantly and report suspicious products.
